# Gepromed — Brand DNA (Source of Truth)

> Canonical brand reference for all Gepromed AI skills and assets.
> Every Gepromed skill should align its output to this file.

**Brand in one line:** GEPROMED is a safety-first medical-device expertise hub,
built around the full implant lifecycle: testing, training, clinical research,
and explant analysis. It should feel like a **trusted scientific safety
authority**, not a healthcare marketing brand.

---

## 1. Core idea & positioning

| Layer | DNA |
|---|---|
| **Core idea** | Medical-device safety through the full implant cycle. |
| **Mission** | Improve patient safety by creating feedback loops between clinicians, engineers, researchers, manufacturers, and institutions. |
| **Positioning** | A multidisciplinary medical-device hub at the crossroads of academia, clinicians, engineering, industry, and public health. |
| **Brand promise** | Safer care, better device knowledge, better-trained professionals, and earlier detection of risks linked to implantable devices. |
| **One-line essence** | Engineering safer medical-device care across the implant lifecycle. |
| **Short positioning** | GEPROMED is a medical-device safety hub combining testing, training, clinical research, and explant analysis to improve patient care. |
| **Brand archetype** | The Guardian + The Expert — protect patients through technical knowledge. |

**Do NOT position as:** a generic medical association · a training center only ·
a lab only · a commercial device company.

## 2. The four pillars

Every page, asset, and skill output should connect back to one of these:

1. **Testing** — mechanical / biomaterial testing of cardiovascular devices.
2. **Education** — simulation-based surgical training (René Kieny Education Center).
3. **Clinical Research** — device surveillance and clinical studies.
4. **Explant Analysis** — collecting and analyzing explants to close the loop.

## 3. Proof points (use to establish authority)

- +1150 healthcare professionals trained since 2018
- +150 vascular explants received in 2023
- +20 clinical research studies
- ISO 9001, ISO 13485, and Qualiopi certified
- World's largest database of vascular explants
- Heritage since 1993 (formerly GEPROVAS)

## 4. Values

Independence · transparency · openness · sharing · ethics · humanism ·
safe care · risk prevention · continuous improvement · scientific rigor.

## 5. Personality & emotional territory

- **Personality:** scientific, serious, institutional, precise, collaborative,
  safety-obsessed, non-commercial.
- **Emotional territory:** trust, vigilance, protection, mastery, continuous
  improvement.
- **Functional territory:** testing, certification, simulation, education,
  clinical research, device surveillance, explant analysis.

## 6. Tone of voice

Expert, evidence-led, calm, technical, public-health oriented. **Avoid hype.**

## 7. Audiences

Surgeons · healthcare professionals · researchers · medical-device
manufacturers · public institutions · funders · academic partners.

## 8. Strategic tension (known weakness to fix)

The brand has strong scientific credibility, but website copy reads translated
and sometimes unclear, which weakens authority. **The DNA is strong; the
expression needs tightening.** Default fix: make everything revolve around one
clean idea — *Medical-device safety across the implant cycle* — and tie each
piece back to one of the four pillars.

## 9. Logo DNA

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Wordmark** | Large uppercase = authority, institutional seriousness, technical confidence. |
| **Tagline** | "The medical device hub" + "For patient safety" makes positioning explicit and fast to grasp. |
| **Blue** | Dominant medical/scientific trust color. Healthcare, reliability, precision, institutional credibility. |
| **Orange accent** | The orange "O" is a focal point: innovation, vigilance, alertness, intervention, the "cycle" idea. |
| **Orange "O" symbol** | Strongest asset — can read as implant, lens, inspection point, loop, or safety cycle. Treat as a recurring brand device. |
| **Horizontal lines** | Structure and technical framing — makes the logo feel like a scientific label / institutional seal. |
| **Typography feel** | Geometric, sans-serif, uppercase, engineered. |
| **Overall impression** | Clinical, credible, specialized, safety-driven. Not warm or emotional — appropriate for the category. |

## 10. Visual DNA to keep

- **Blue as the master color** — owns medical trust and scientific seriousness.
- **Orange as a rare accent** — warning / safety / innovation marker, not decoration.
- **The orange "O" / cycle motif** — most ownable asset; use in diagrams, icons,
  section markers, and the implant-cycle visual system.
- **Clean white space** — supports credibility and medical clarity.
- **Technical diagrams over stock imagery** — matches real strength: expertise,
  systems, devices, process, safety.
