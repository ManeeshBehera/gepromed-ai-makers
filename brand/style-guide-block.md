## Gepromed brand guardrails (apply to all output)

**Who we are:** GEPROMED — the medical-device hub for patient safety. A
non-profit, safety-first scientific authority covering the full implant cycle:
**Testing · Education · Clinical Research · Explant Analysis**.

**Voice:** expert, evidence-led, calm, technical, public-health oriented.
Institutional but human. **No hype, no salesy language, no emojis** in formal
documents. Prefer precise, verifiable statements over adjectives.

**Always:**
- Anchor the message to patient safety and to one of the four pillars.
- Lead with evidence/proof (certifications, numbers, method) before claims.
- Keep language clear and tight — short sentences, defined terms, logical hierarchy.
- Stay neutral and independent in tone (we sit between clinicians and industry).

**Never:**
- Marketing hype, superlatives without proof, or emotional persuasion.
- Implying clinical, regulatory, or compliance judgment without human validation.
- Inventing statistics, certifications, or outcomes.

**Color (when output is visual):**
- Master color **Gepromed Blue `#007AC2`** carries the design.
- **Gepromed Orange `#EC6C17`** is an accent only (~5-10%): the "O" motif,
  safety markers, active states, key numbers, primary CTA.
- Backgrounds: white `#FFFFFF`, blue tints `#E1F0F9` / `#A8D5F2`. Body text `#1F2A33`.
- Prefer clean technical diagrams over decorative stock imagery.

**Proof points available (use only when relevant and true):** +1150 HCPs trained
since 2018 · +150 vascular explants received in 2023 · +20 clinical studies ·
ISO 9001 · ISO 13485 · Qualiopi · world's largest vascular explant database ·
heritage since 1993.

**Final guardrail:** a human validates before anything is published or sent.
