# GEPROMED email reformulation — worked examples

Real before→after rewrites showing the target quality. Study the *moves*, not
just the words: language mirrored, intent preserved, facts untouched, hype
removed, one clear ask, recipient-appropriate register, unknowns bracketed.

---

## Example 1 — FR · follow-up to a training participant (warm-professional)

**Before (raw):**
> bonjour, suite a votre demande pour la formation bootcamp vasculaire je voulais
> savoir si vous etes toujours interessé?? on a encore de la place. dites moi
> vite svp sinon ça part. merci

**After:**
```
Subject: Votre place au Bootcamp Vasculaire — confirmation

Bonjour [Prénom],

Suite à votre demande d'inscription au Bootcamp Vasculaire, je reviens vers vous
pour savoir si vous souhaitez confirmer votre participation.

Des places sont encore disponibles pour la session du [date]. Si vous le
souhaitez, je peux vous réserver la vôtre — il me faudrait simplement votre
confirmation avant le [date limite].

Je reste à votre disposition pour toute question sur le programme ou la logistique.

Bien cordialement,
[Nom]
GEPROMED — René Kieny Education Center
```
**Moves:** removed pressure ("sinon ça part"), kept the real facts, added a
specific deadline as a `[placeholder]`, warm but not pushy, concrete next step.

## Example 2 — EN · outreach to a medical-device manufacturer (independent, evidence-led)

**Before (raw):**
> Hi, we are GEPROMED, a world-class leader in explant analysis 🚀 and we would
> love to partner with your amazing company to test your devices, we are the best
> in europe!! let me know asap

**After:**
```
Subject: Independent explant analysis & mechanical testing — GEPROMED

Dear [First name],

I am reaching out from GEPROMED, a non-profit medical-device hub focused on
patient safety. We run independent explant analysis and mechanical/biomaterial
testing on cardiovascular devices, under ISO 9001 and ISO 13485.

If it would be useful, I would be glad to share how our testing and explant
analysis could support your device evaluation work, and to explore whether a
collaboration makes sense.

Would you be open to a short call in the coming weeks?

Best regards,
[Name]
GEPROMED — Test Platform
```
**Moves:** stripped hype and emoji, replaced "best/world-class" with verifiable
facts (ISO, non-profit, independent), one clear ask, neutral partner tone.

## Example 3 — FR · supplier chase (direct, courteous, firm)

**Before (raw):**
> rebonjour, ça fait 2 semaines que j'attend le devis pour les consommables, je
> vous l'ai demandé plusieurs fois, c'est urgent maintenant.

**After:**
```
Subject: Relance — devis consommables (réf. [référence])

Bonjour,

Je me permets de revenir vers vous concernant le devis pour les consommables
demandé le [date]. Nous en avons besoin pour [usage / échéance].

Pourriez-vous me le transmettre d'ici le [date] ? Sans retour de votre part à
cette date, nous devrons envisager une autre solution pour tenir notre planning.

Je vous remercie par avance.

Cordialement,
[Nom]
GEPROMED
```
**Moves:** firmness via a stated consequence + deadline, not irritation; added a
reference placeholder; courteous throughout.

## Example 4 — EN · institution / funder (formal, mission-forward)

**Before (raw):**
> Dear team, just wanted to quickly update you that the training went super well,
> loads of people came, and we'd love more funding to do more of this great stuff.

**After:**
```
Subject: [Programme] outcomes and continued support

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to share the outcomes of our recent [programme] and to discuss its
continuation.

The session trained [N] healthcare professionals in [specialty], contributing to
GEPROMED's mission of improving patient safety through simulation-based surgical
education. [Add one concrete outcome / satisfaction figure if available.]

Building on these results, we would welcome the opportunity to discuss continued
support to extend the programme to [scope]. I would be glad to provide a detailed
report and to meet at your convenience.

Yours faithfully,
[Name]
GEPROMED
```
**Moves:** formal register and closing, vague enthusiasm replaced by bracketed
real metrics, funding ask framed around mission and impact.

## Example 5 — FR → EN · translate + adapt for a surgeon (peer register)

**Before (raw, FR):**
> Cher docteur, merci d'avoir participé à notre étude sur les explants, vos
> données sont très utiles, est-ce qu'on peut vous recontacter pour la suite?

**After (EN):**
```
Subject: Thank you for contributing to our explant study

Dear Dr [Name],

Thank you for taking part in our explant analysis study — your data is a valuable
contribution to better understanding device complications and improving patient
safety.

As we move to the next phase, may we contact you to discuss continued
involvement? I would be happy to share where the work is heading.

Kind regards,
[Name]
GEPROMED — Clinical Research
```
**Moves:** faithful translation, peer-to-peer surgeon register, tied to the
patient-safety mission, one clear ask, no added claims.

## Anti-pattern reference (what NOT to produce)
- Keeping "🚀", "world-class", "amazing", "best in Europe", "super well".
- Uniform sentence length that reads machine-generated.
- A polite rewrite with **no subject line** and **no explicit ask**.
- Inventing numbers ("we trained 2000 people") instead of bracketing `[N]`.
- English closings on a French email, or institutional formality on an internal note.
