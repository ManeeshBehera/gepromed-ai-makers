# Gepromed — Tone & Voice Validation Checklist

Run any skill output against this checklist before it is considered "done".
Each skill can embed this as its self-check / human-review gate.

## A. On-brand voice
- [ ] Reads as an **expert scientific authority**, not a marketing brand.
- [ ] **Calm, evidence-led, technical** — no hype, no superlatives without proof.
- [ ] Neutral and **independent** in tone (between clinicians and industry).
- [ ] Clear hierarchy, short sentences, defined terms; no translated-sounding copy.
- [ ] No emojis or casual phrasing in formal/scientific documents.

## B. On-brand substance
- [ ] Anchored to **patient safety**.
- [ ] Tied to one of the four pillars: **Testing / Education / Clinical Research / Explant Analysis**.
- [ ] Leads with **proof** (method, certification, numbers) before any claim.
- [ ] Uses only **true, verifiable** facts — no invented stats, certs, or outcomes.

## C. Visual (if the output is visual)
- [ ] **Blue `#007AC2`** is the dominant color.
- [ ] **Orange `#EC6C17`** used as accent only (~5-10%), not decoration.
- [ ] Generous white space; technical diagrams preferred over stock imagery.
- [ ] The orange "O" / cycle motif used where a brand device fits.

## D. Compliance & safety gate
- [ ] No clinical, regulatory, RGPD/GDPR, or Qualiopi **judgment** is automated.
- [ ] Sensitive/regulated content flags the **named human validator**.
- [ ] **A human validates before publishing or sending.**

## E. Do-not-position check
- [ ] Not framed as a generic medical association, a training center only, a lab
      only, or a commercial device company.
